1. Who we are

Taash Adda ("the app", "we") is a free card-game app. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. If you have questions, write to {{EMAIL}}.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your own phone (browser/app local storage): the nickname and avatar you choose, game scores and statistics, in-game virtual gems/coins, badges, settings (language, theme, sound), and a random reconnect token that lets you return to an online game after a lost connection. We do not receive this data unless you play online (see below). Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes it.

3. Information used when you play online

When you use online multiplayer, the app sends your nickname, avatar, the room code, and your game moves (bids and cards played) to our game server so that other players in the room can see them. This information is kept only in the server's memory while the room is active and is discarded when the room ends or after a period of inactivity. We do not write it to a database or keep it permanently. Nicknames and chat-style emoji reactions are visible to the other players in your room, so please do not use your real full name or personal details as a nickname.

4. Technical data

Our hosting provider (Render) may automatically process technical data such as IP address and request logs to deliver the service and keep it secure. We do not use this data to identify you or to build profiles.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not show ads and do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not collect payments. Gems/coins are virtual items inside the game, have no real-world value, and cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for money or prizes. The app is not a gambling or real-money game.

6. Children

Taash Adda is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

7. Your choices

You can delete all locally stored data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Because we do not keep online game data after a room ends, there is nothing further to delete on our side. For any request, write to {{EMAIL}}.

8. Security

Online communication uses an encrypted (HTTPS/WSS) connection. No method of transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the service.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app changes (for example, if sign-in with a phone number or Google account is added in a future version), we will update this policy and the "last updated" date above before the new feature is released.
